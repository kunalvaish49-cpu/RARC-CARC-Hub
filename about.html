About RARC & CARC Intelligence Hub

Welcome to the most comprehensive resource for RARC (Remittance Advice Remark Codes) and CARC (Claim Adjustment Reason Codes) information. Our mission is to simplify healthcare billing and claims management through clear, actionable intelligence.

What We Do

RARC & CARC Intelligence Hub is a specialized platform dedicated to helping healthcare billing professionals, coding specialists, and claims managers understand and resolve billing denials quickly and efficiently. We provide:

  • Complete RARC & CARC Database: Detailed explanations of every remittance advice and claim adjustment code.
  • Root Cause Analysis: Understand why claims are being denied and how to prevent them.
  • Correction Workflows: Step-by-step guidance to resolve billing issues.
  • Payer-Specific Rules: Insurance company-specific billing requirements and guidelines.
  • Modifier Reference Guide: Complete information on CPT and HCPCS modifiers.
  • Interactive Note Maker: Built-in tool for creating detailed billing notes and documentation.
